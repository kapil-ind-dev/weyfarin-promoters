Three weeks across Ladakh, and what I'd do differently

Posted 14 September 2026

The Markha Valley is the trek everyone recommends first, and for once the consensus is right. Six days, two passes, and homestays that cost less than a coffee in Delhi. What nobody tells you is how much the acclimatisation days at the start decide whether the whole thing is enjoyable or survivable.

If you are planning something similar, these are the operators I ended up booking through. I get a small cut if you book, which I mention because you should assume that about every travel blog you read.

Back to the trek. The second pass, Kongmaru La at 5,260 m, is the one that breaks people. Start before five. The descent into Chokdo is longer than the map suggests and there is no shade for the last two hours.
